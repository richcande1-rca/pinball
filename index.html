Instructions Build 20260829-CENTERTARGETS

Flippers: Z and / (or ← and →). Plunger: hold Space or LAUNCH; medium enters playfield, strong takes the upper-right ramp. Returned launches are replayable; live balls entering the shooter lane feed the right flipper. Reset: R. Cradle a ball to charge its flipper glow; release, aim, then flip again for a powered shot. Magnetic target: 500 points. Complete the upper-left loop: 2500 points. Loop bumpers score escalating 100, 200, and 300-point volleys. Captive ball: 500 points per solid hit; reach its top switch for 2500 more; five solid hits light all five lamps and award one extra ball. Complete the three center standups to start 18 seconds of 2X scoring. Knock down 3-0-5 for 500 each and a 3000-point bank bonus; completing the bank blocks the left outlane until that ball drains. Take the elevated OCEAN DRIVE ramp for 100 points per spinner rotation. Shoot the upper pocket to enter the five-way underpass. Three balls per game; NEW GAME or R restarts. Cabinet buttons work with touch, mouse, or pen.
